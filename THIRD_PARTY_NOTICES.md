# Third-party assets

## Font Awesome Free 6.4.0

The site uses Font Awesome Free solid icons by Fonticons, Inc. The included CSS and web font are in `assets/vendor/fontawesome/`. Font Awesome license information is included in `assets/licenses/Font-Awesome-LICENSE.txt`.

Project: https://fontawesome.com/
License: https://fontawesome.com/license/free

## Inter and Orbitron

The site includes Inter (v20) and Orbitron (v35) web fonts sourced from Google Fonts. Their local CSS and WOFF2 files are in `assets/fonts/`. Both fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license texts are included in `assets/licenses/Inter-OFL.txt` and `assets/licenses/Orbitron-OFL.txt`.

Inter: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter
Orbitron: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Orbitron
